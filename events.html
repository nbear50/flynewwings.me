Events

Capture events like never before

Camps & Retreats

Camp is something kids remember for years, so I try to capture all of it with the drone. That kind of footage helps get more kids excited to come back next summer. And yes, I can edit A Full Commercial for your event.

Parades & Festivals

Parades and festivals are hard to film from the ground because there's so much going on. I fly above it so you can see the whole crowd, the floats, the band, all at once. Ask me about getting 2 Separate Angles using 2 drones.

Action Sports

Sports move fast and it's hard to keep up from the sidelines. I fly FPV next to the action so you get shots a normal camera on the ground will never get. I can record up to 2 Consecutive Hours of footage from any angle.

Private Parties

Weddings, reunions, parties, these are days people actually want to remember. I'll get you the kind of shots you'll actually want to look back on. Great drone footage is often paired with my just as incredible Ground Level Footage. I love shooting parties because everyone is pumped up which makes the footage amazing.
