What Else I've Been Up To...

Woodworking

Learning the art of woodworking with the help of friends and my dad. My favorite projects have been a fruit bowl and a red oak nightstand.

Purdue Engineering

In the ENGR131 Ideas to Innovation class, I worked as a team to design a fully interactive game for students based on the Purdue Bell Tower. The game featured a hammer that launched a ball up the inside of the tower, in an attempt to ring the bells at the top.

Boiler Royale

THE Outdoor Public Clash Royale Tournament at Purdue University. Players went head to head in Clash Royale with matches projected on the big screen and the bracket managed by my own custom software. With over 100 competitors and a 150$ cash prize it was a night for the books for sure.
